How it works
Over 1: Monitors digit frequency for 0, 1, 2. When any % rises above threshold → arms. On the next tick where that exact digit appears → places Over 1 contract.

Martingale: After each loss, next stake = base × 4.5 (one step only). Resets to base after the martingale trade wins or loses.

How Stable Market Scan works:
1. Fetches the last N ticks per selected market.
2. Computes digit frequency (0–9) across those ticks.
3. Calculates spread = max% − min% across all 10 digits.
4. A stable market has a small spread (digits are evenly distributed — no runaway bias).
5. Picks the market with the lowest spread and applies it to the Bot.
